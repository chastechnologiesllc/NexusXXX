Law Enforcement & Legal Requests

Last updated: August 15, 2026

NexusXXX cooperates with law enforcement and responds to valid legal process.

How to Submit a Request

Law enforcement agencies seeking information or preservation should submit a formal request (subpoena, court order, or search warrant as applicable) via the Contact page, marked “Law Enforcement – Official Request”.

Include:

  • Agency name and case number
  • Requesting officer contact details
  • Specific information sought and legal basis
  • Any urgency / preservation request

Emergency Disclosure

In cases involving imminent risk of death or serious physical injury, contact us immediately through the same channel and clearly mark the request as an emergency.

User Privacy

We disclose user information only when required by valid legal process or in emergency situations consistent with applicable law. We may notify users of requests unless prohibited by law or court order.

Non-Law-Enforcement Legal Notices

Civil process and copyright notices should follow the procedures in our DMCA Policy and Terms.
